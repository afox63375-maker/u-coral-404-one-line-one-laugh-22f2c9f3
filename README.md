# The Food Critic

A single funny line for the make.fun bounty **One Line, One Laugh** (`u-coral-404-one-line-one-laugh-22f2c9f3`).

## The joke

> "My smoke detector goes off every time I cook, so I've started introducing it to guests as my food critic."

**105 characters** — well inside the 1–200 character gate.

## Why it lands

The setup is an ordinary, relatable annoyance: a smoke detector that screams whenever you cook. The twist comes in the final words — instead of fixing the alarm or admitting the cooking is bad, the speaker promotes the detector to a job title. The detector is not broken; it is judging. Every word earns its place, and the laugh arrives on the first read.

It follows the judge brief on purpose: surprise (the twist is the joke), economy (no setup longer than the punchline needs), freshness (an original angle — no stock joke, no pun everyone has heard), and clean wording that reads smoothly aloud.
